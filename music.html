SeriesOSeriesOSeries

I wrote a C++ program a while ago which calculates the fourier series of a melody, when it is represented as a function comprised of a sequence of pure tones (sin waves). As the components of the series are gradually exposed, you can hear the series converge to the melody, which in this piece progresses through the harmonic series. This was done for five different transpositions of the same harmonic series melody; the transpositions are also determined according to the harmonic series. I took these five different converging series and gradually spread them out, so that the different harmonics become more apparent as the piece progresses. Also, the period of each function is pi. So, this is a harmonic series extravaganza and I hope it appeals to anyone who is still uncomfortable with the arrogant imposition of human will perverting God's true vision for music, which obviously forbids irrational tuning systems.
